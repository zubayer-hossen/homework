About Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a beautiful country in South Asia. Dhaka is the capital city of Bangladesh. The country is famous for its natural beauty, rivers, green countryside and friendly people.

Bangladesh became independent in 1971. The national language is Bengali and the national flag is green with a red circle.
